**Last updated: October 4, 2026**

TimeFlow is provided free of charge by Rinse Repeat Labs. There are no accounts, subscriptions or in-app purchases.

## Your data is yours, and it's on your device

Your tasks are stored on your own device. You're responsible for keeping backups (Settings → Back up tasks); we can't recover data that's lost with a device.

## Reminders

Reminders depend on your device's notification settings, and we can't guarantee delivery. Don't rely on TimeFlow for medical or safety-critical timing.

## Share links

Share links contain the schedule you choose to share. Anyone with the link can see it, so share it only with people you trust.
